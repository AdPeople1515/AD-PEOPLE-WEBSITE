Why Choose ACP

PRODUCT ADVANTAGES

01

Flat & Rigid Surface

Extremely flat panel surface — ideal for printing, signage and any use where surface consistency matters. Minimal warpage (≤5mm/m).

02

Lightweight

Significantly lighter than glass, stone or solid aluminium. Reduces structural load and makes installation faster and easier on-site.

03

Weather & UV Resistant

PVDF-coated panels designed to withstand Singapore's tropical climate — high humidity, UV exposure, rain and heat.

04

Easy to Fabricate

Can be cut, routed, bent, drilled and folded using standard woodworking or metal tools. No specialist equipment required.

05

Fire-Rated Option

Mineral FR core available for applications requiring fire-retardant performance — meets building and MRT specifications.

06

Printable Surface

Smooth, consistent surface accepts UV flatbed printing directly — ideal for murals, branded fascias and display panels.

Handling Guide

INSTALLATION TIPS

1

Install in one direction

Always install all panels in a single direction as marked on the protective film. This prevents colour shade or reflection differences between panels on the same surface.

2

Remove protective film promptly

Remove immediately after installation — do not leave on panel for more than 45 days to prevent adhesive residue in Singapore's hot and humid weather.

3

Use simple tools for fabrication

Panels are lightweight and easy to cut using standard woodworking or metal tools. CNC routing is suitable for channel letters, shaped fascias and complex designs.

4

Clean with mild soap only

Use water and a mild soap solution for regular cleaning. Do not use abrasive or corrosive cleaning materials — these will damage the PVDF or PE surface coating.
